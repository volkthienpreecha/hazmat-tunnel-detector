# Product

## Register
product

## Users
MECH100 student team operating an inspection rover from a laptop or phone.

## Product Purpose
Observe environmental readings, drive a prototype rover, and demonstrate a
bounded autonomous hazard-response routine before hardware integration.

## Brand Personality
Precise, restrained, watchful. User references: Palantir instrumentation,
the supplied Worldview screenshot, Apple glass controls and Unslop.

## Anti-references
Generic marketing dashboards, fabricated camera/map views, decorative metric
cards, unreadable neon effects and claims that measurements establish safe entry.

## Design Principles
Prioritize stopping and hazards. Distinguish requested outputs from telemetry.
Expose measurement freshness. Keep simulation explicit. Work without internet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Builder baseline: semantic controls, text plus color status, visible keyboard
focus, responsive phone controls, reduced motion and transparency support.
No additional user-specific accessibility requirements were supplied.
