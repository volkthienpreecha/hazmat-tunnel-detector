# Field console design

Dark graphite instruments for an operator at a dim mock-tunnel entrance.
Restrained cyan selection and gas series; amber/red carry hazard meaning.
System sans-serif with tabular monospace figures, 4 px spacing rhythm,
hairlines and solid data surfaces. Glass belongs to the header and manual
control layer, with opaque fallback. Main gas trend is the visual focus.
No location mapping is implied by the top-view vehicle schematic.

Use Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (CC BY 4.0) for craft review. Requested Apple-style
controls override its general preference to avoid glass; effects remain sparse.
See docs/references/apple-materials-notes.md for official source and application.
